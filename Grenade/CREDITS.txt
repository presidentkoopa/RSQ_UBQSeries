RSQ Grenade -- credits
======================

The Radiant Silvergun Quest series' VR hand grenade, for stock QuestZDoom 17.3: ZScript and lumps only, no engine
changes. The code is written for this pack (presidentkoopa's series); what it ports and what it ships is below.

WHAT THIS PK3 SHIPS, AND WHOSE IT IS
- UAC Survival Pack, by JohnnyDoom (forum.zdoom.org, topic t=47229; the copy the owner supplied,
  UAC_SurvivalPack.pk3). Its hand grenade: original by Froon, Brutal Doom version by Saegiru, sprites by Pa1nki113r
  (the credits at the head of its DECORATE.grenade).
  From it, unchanged except for the lump names (renamed so no other mod's lumps are replaced):
  - the sounds (sounds/rsqgn/): GBANG1-8 -> QGBANG1-8 (the blast), FAREXPL -> QGFAREXP (the distant blast),
    DSGRPIN -> QGPIN (pin pull, pickup), DSGRTOSS -> QGTOSS (throw), DSGRDROP -> QGSPOON (the spoon flying off),
    GBOUNCE -> QGBOUNCE (bounce), GPIN -> QGTICK (the fuse tick); volumes and roll-off from its SNDINFO.grenade.
    The pack names no author for its individual sounds.
  - the pickup sprites: JGNDD0 -> QGN1A0 (one grenade), MGRND0 -> QGN3A0 (a pack of three); the thrown grenade's
    sprites JGRNA0-H0 -> QGNTA0-H0 (shown only with models off).
  - the explosion's sprites (sprites/rsqgn/QGXP*, QGSM*, QGSK*, QGSC*, QGLR*, QGL2*, QGFL*: its JXPL, JSMO, JSPK,
    JS17, JLNR, JL2N, JFLR) and the explosion itself (J_ThrownGrenade's XDeath, transcribed in the owner's RS_Grenade
    rs_blast.zs and ported here as zscript/rsqgn/blast.zs).
  - its ammo rules (carry 8, backpack +6 to 16, a single and a pack of three, their pickup messages) and its random
    placement of grenades beside each map's items (randomuac.acs UAC_RandomItemSpawner), rewritten in ZScript.
- Gomuz: the grenade model (models/rsqgn/nade.md3 and nade.png), "Grenade by Gomuz" in the owner's BD22 VR Weapons
  credits; the same files as in his BD22 VR Reloaded and RS_Grenade. models/rsqgn/nade_red.png is that skin
  recoloured red (tools/gen_red.py) for the fuse flash.
- The project owner (presidentkoopa):
  - The handling: his Brutal Doom 22 VR Reloaded grenade (RS_Brutal22WeaponsQ, zscript/rsg/grenade.zs) -- the off hand
    pulls the ring, the trigger is the spoon, let go swinging to throw, let go still to cook, cook too long and it goes
    off -- ported to this pack's own weapon, with its held-grenade placement (MODELDEF.rsq HandGrenades, lane B's
    fit_nade.py) and its measured ring and body points. The clear surface skin (models/rsqgn/rsqgn_clear.png) is
    that pack's rsr_clear.png.
  - His RS_Grenade (rs_vrgrenade.zs, rs_blast.zs): pulling the pin at your mouth (the two-radius face route), the red
    flash that speeds up with the fuse, the flight numbers (gravity 0.27, bounce 0.5 / 0.25, ground drag 0.82, the
    tumble, the arc), the blast's damage (85 over 200, then 75 over 255) and its port of the pack's explosion.
- Generated for this pack: the transparent placeholder sprites QGNHA0-D0 (the held grenade is drawn as a model only).
- QuestZDoom (Emawind and Team Beef): the engine, and the off-hand weapon and hand-pose script API this reads.

SOUNDS
Every sound is the UAC Survival Pack's own recording. Nothing is synthesized. The Gun Bonsai VR tree's (below) are not.

THE GRENADE TREE'S EFFECTS AND SOUNDS (Gun Bonsai VR patch, 2026-11-02)
  From the owner's two wells, RS Combat FX and RS Ballistics (presidentkoopa's libraries), one asset at a time, under
  this pack's own names (QGB1 QGBB QGIC QGFI QGCL QGHF; QGBOMB1-3 QGBIGB QGFREEZ QGFIRE QGSMOKE QGFLASH QGSTUN QGCHARM):
  Grenade/tools/fxpull.py is the record of each pull (the well and the path); sounds converted to mono 44.1 kHz.
  The kinds' skins (models/rsqgn/nade_<kind>.png) are Gomuz's nade.png recoloured by tools/gen_kinds.py.

THE BARRELS (barrel.zs, 2026-11-09): six barrel explosions, from the owner's approved Python designs (the record of
every layer: Grenade/tools/barrel_spec/, exported by tools/barrel_export.py into sprites/rsqgn/barrel/ QB** and QX**
and zscript/rsqgn/barrel_data.zs). Every sprite is the source art recoloured, re-centred and trimmed, at its own pixel
size; nothing is smoothed.
  Sources and their terms:
  - RS Combat FX (presidentkoopa, the owner's own pack): the pixel embers RSS0A0-D0 (Monsters/sprites/combatfx/
    sparks), raw (#5 #6 #7 #8), through the dirty-fire ramp (#9), through the neon ramp (#10): QXEA, QXEB, QXEC. And
    every barrel sound (sounds/rsqgn/barrel/, copied unchanged from RS Monsters' copy): explosions/BA_EXP01-03 ->
    QGBX1-3 (the blast), explosions/DISTEXP1-5 -> QGBD1-5 (the distant blast), magdrops/DSBOUNC1-6 -> QGBC1-6 (the
    debris clatter).
  - Reactorcore, "Explosion Spriteanim Sheet Minipack" (https://reactorcore.itch.io/explosion-spriteanim-sheet-minipack,
    design/assets/fx_sheets/reactorcore_minipack): CC0 BY A PAGE COMMENT ONLY -- the pack's own info file states no
    licence; the author answered "CC0" in a comment on its page. Get it in writing (or a licence file) to be sure.
    Used: the ring flash (#5 main, #8 children), sheet 4 fireballs (#5 #6 #7 children, #7 column; #9 brown
    fireballs), the red fireball to smoke (#6 body, #7 top, #9 bottom child), the grey puff (the smoke and soot of all
    six; #10's through a purple ramp), sheet 10 (#9 main blast), sheet 3 crescents (#10).
  - Rufaswan's rips, sheet by Magma MK-II (design/assets/fx_sheets/fx_sheet_rufaswan_magma.png). On the sheet:
    "Credits not necessary." Credited anyway. Used: the yellow flash ring (#5 #7 floor rings, #7 main, #8 main and
    shock ring, #10 gold ring), the fire ring (#6 child, #7 crown, #8 ember scorch), the streak flare (the three pops

  - fx_sheet_2.png (design/assets/fx_sheets): *** SOURCE UNKNOWN *** -- the owner kept it (fx_sheets/README.md:
    "ask the owner before shipping"). Used ONLY in #6 Heavy: its main blast (the flash domes, gradient-mapped to
    fire: sprites QB01X-Z, QB02A-B) and its floor ring (f2ring: QB02H), as exported on 11-09. If this sheet
    cannot ship, #6 needs a new main blast and floor ring; the other five do not use it.
