RSQ FnF (Flash and Flare) -- credits
=====================================

The Radiant Silvergun Quest series' VR road flare and flashlight, for stock QuestZDoom 17.3: ZScript and lumps
only, no engine changes. The code is written for this pack (presidentkoopa's series).

WHAT THIS PK3 SHIPS, AND WHOSE IT IS
- The flare model: the project owner's flare (Flare.blend with FlareTexture.png and FlareTip.png, from his flare.zip),
  read straight from the .blend by tools/blendread.py and turned into models/rsqff/flare.md3, flare_h.md3 and box.md3
  by tools/gen_flare.py (scaled to a 22.5 cm road flare). Its skins are those two textures, resized.
- The hand: Brutal Doom 22 VR Reloaded's FBX hand (the owner's mod, RS_Brutal22WeaponsQ): its off-hand rifle-magazine
  hold (models/rsq/fbx/fbx_oh_hold_R.md3 frame 0) as models/rsqff/rsqff_fbxhand.md3, and its skin fbx_hand.png (resized).
  Drawn in BD22 VR's own off-hand MODELDEF placement, as RSQ Saber's Force hand does.
- Generated for this pack (tools/gen_flare.py): the glowing end (ember.png), the flame, spark and smoke particle
  pictures (graphics/rsqff/), the clear skin and the transparent placeholder sprites.
- UDV Survival Flares (saegiru's Emergency Flare), part of the UAC Survival Pack by JohnnyDoom (forum.zdoom.org, topic
  t=47229; the copies the owner supplied, Gameplay_UDV_Survival_Flares.pk3 and UAC_SurvivalPack.pk3). From it, unchanged
  except for the lump names (renamed so no other mod's lumps are replaced):
  - the box sprite FBOXA0 -> QFFXA0 (the box of 6; offsets 14 16, as shipped) and the lying flare FLRLA0 -> QFFBA0 (one
    flare; offsets set to 11 11, its bottom centre). Drawn at his scales (FlareBox 0.7, the flare 0.5).
  - the sounds (sounds/rsqff/): FLRSTRT -> QFFSTRT (the strike), FLRBURN -> QFFBURN (the burning, looped), FLROUT ->
    QFFOUT (burnt out). The pack names no author for its individual sounds.
- BD_VoxelPack (the owner's load order, 02_BD22_VoxelPack.pk3; its Credits.txt): DooMAD's "Ammo Box" voxel
  (Voxels/Ammo/AMMOA.kvx) made into the flare box voxel voxels/QFFBOX.kvx by tools/gen_flarebox.py: its bullet belt and
  open lid flap taken off, the lid closed, the two panels of each side made one, recoloured to saegiru's FBOXA0 (its
  shading kept), "FLARE" and a latch put on its long sides. Shown under its own sprite name, QFFX A (VOXELDEF.txt), so
  it never stands in for BD_VoxelPack's own. The pack's voxel authors, as its Credits.txt names them: Ghostpilot,
  DooMAD, Monfriez, DooMGoaT, Exl (its ZDoom fork, offset utility), Scott_AW (Strip2Voxel), ReaperMan, 4mer, phi108,
  Conrad RDW, Voxelbro, zedas.
- The flashlight (tools/gen_torch.py): the hand is Brutal Doom 22 VR Reloaded's (the owner's mod) shotgun pump / forend
  support hand, models/rsq/fbx/fbx_s192_L.md3 frame 1, copied as models/rsqff/rsqff_pumphand.md3 (moved, not turned) with
  its skin fbx_hand.png, drawn in BD22 VR's own off-hand MODELDEF placement. The flashlight itself, its beam cones, their
  skins and the hotspot sprite are generated for this pack. Ideas from Ermac's VR-Flashlight (github.com/iAmErmac,
  base code SuaveSteve's "Steve's Flashlight": a spot light that follows the controller) and DarkDoomz (Caligari87,
  zlib: a spot light's look). The flashlight's BATTERY (2026-11-06, zscript/rsqff/battery.zs) follows Ermac's model as
  his zscript/flashlight.txt has it (read, rewritten here, no code copied): capacity in minutes (his default 3, 0 = never
  drains), drain while on and recharge while off at the same rate, his 10-step level with a blinking LED at the last step,
  and his low-battery dim and flicker numbers. The meter's art (models/rsqff/meter0..10.png, torch_meter.md3; tools/
  gen_torch.py --meter) is our own, drawn after his battery_0..10.png meter; none of his assets is used.
- Sounds otherwise: stock Doom sounds by their own names (SNDINFO.txt aliases): the platform stop (landing), the item
  pickup (pocket), the switch (the flashlight's click).

IDEAS AND NUMBERS FROM
- saegiru's Emergency Flare (UDV Survival Flares, part of JohnnyDoom's UAC Survival Pack; DECORATE.flares,
  GLDEFS.flares, randomuac.acs): a box of 6, carry 24; the red-orange light (colour 1.0 0.3 0.0) raised off the floor;
  boxes rolled beside a map's items (0-5 of 126, up to a cap a map); a thrown flare that bounces and lies burning, with
