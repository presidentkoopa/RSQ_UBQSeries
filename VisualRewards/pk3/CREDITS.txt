RSQ VisualRewards -- credits
============================

RSQ VisualRewards (2026-11-03) is RSQ HP Bars (the HP bars, the damage numbers, the level-up displays) and Kill
Rewards (until then inside RSQ Gun Bonsai VR) in one pk3. HP BARS: coldcite (below). DAMAGE NUMBERS: Xaser Acheron
(below). KILL REWARDS: SoM (below). FONTS: JFO by Zedas / Blackmore1014 (licence note below), Ermac's digits.


Top HP Bars 1.04 by coldcite (2017). The layout, the bar art (both styles, all 44 frames), the
default look and the idea are his; Classic and Red ship his images byte for byte, and every other
colour set is his style-A art recoloured pixel by pixel (tools/gen_art.py in the source repo).
His header: "Feel free to use this in your mods. You don't have to ask my permission!"
Thank you, coldcite.

DAMAGE NUMBER FONTS (2026-11-03):
  - JFO metal digits and letters (the default Number Font, and every word: HEADSHOT, OVERKILL, xN): JFO,
    the JDUI font by Zedas, in the edit "JDUI Brutal Doom Ultimate Edition" by Blackmore1014 (ModDB:
    https://www.moddb.com/addons/jdui-brutal-doom-ultimate-edition). tools/dn_art/jfo_big and jfo_small are
    copied unchanged from RSQ Gun Cards' tools/card_art/jfo (JFO.pk3's Hires/BigFont/fontbNNN.png and
    Hires/SmallFont/stcfnNNN.png); tools/gen_dn_art.py levels their grey and writes the PCVR and Quest
    textures. NO REUSE LICENCE IS STATED for JFO / JDUI: permission should be asked of Zedas and
    Blackmore1014 before any public release (the same note as Gun Cards' CREDITS).
  - Ermac's digits (Number Font: Ermac): iamErmac's Force Unleashed holo HUD digits (digit_*.png), as RSQ Gun
    Cards ships them with the owner's blessing (tools/dn_art/ermac, copied from GunCards/tools/card_art).

Kill Rewards is SoM's "Universal(ish?) Kill Rewards" v1.0 (som_kr): the bits a monster bursts into when it dies,
their three orb sprites (now sprites/rsqkr KRBH / KRBA / KRBM, given a floor offset) and the drop rules. Folded into
RSQ Gun Bonsai VR on 2026-11-02 and moved here on 2026-11-03, with a new plain-words menu and our cvar names
(rsqgb_kr_*). SoM's readme: "Feel free to use anything in this mod for yourself ... I don't need credit, unless you
wanted to." We do. Thank you, SoM.

Not included, credited for the record:
  - Lossvoise's flat high-visibility retexture of Mere_Duke's custom version (zdoom forum
    t=56910) is NOT shipped: no permission yet (credit chain coldcite -> Mere_Duke -> Lossvoise).
  - Champions and LegenDoom Lite: read by class / cvar name for compatibility (handler.zs); nothing of
    either is bundled.

Code: ZScript written for the Ultra Badass Quest series (presidentkoopa), for stock QuestZDoom 17.3
