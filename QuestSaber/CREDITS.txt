UltraBadassQuestSaber -- credits
================================

A volumetric lightsaber for stock QuestZDoom 17.3. No engine changes: everything here runs on the
shipped Quest build.

BORROWED, WITH THANKS
  RS_Main (Ermac -- DXR, Rusted Legacy)
      combat FX sprites, used for the Force and the blade's impacts:
        UXFL  the choke/grip lightning arcs        (RSL2*)
        UXPD  blast smoke -- Force push, and the smoke off a cut   (JSMO*)
        UXFH  the armed-hand flare                 (RSF7*)
      and the Force choke loop:
        ubqs/grip   built from monsters/RSELLOOP.ogg
  QuestZDoom (Emawind)
      the engine this targets, and the off-hand weapon and AttackPos/Angle/Pitch/Roll script API
      the whole mod is built on.
  li-gnrcwpn
      ubqs/rbeep -- the training remote's ready beep, from the plasma rifle's alt-fire alert
      (weapons/plasrifle/PLBEEP).

EVERYTHING ELSE
  The hilt, blade and glow meshes, their skins, and every other sound are this pack's own --
  synthesized or built here, with no sample taken from any film or game.

SHARING
  Free to share as part of the Ultra Badass Quest series. Borrowed assets keep their authors'
  terms: much of Ermac's work is MIT-licensed, and he is DoomVR's co-designer and has okayed its
  use here. (The old "not for distribution" line was carried over from another repo by mistake.)
