RSQ_Miniboss -- credits
=======================

The Radiant Silvergun Quest series' set-piece miniboss. For stock QuestZDoom 17.3: ZScript and lumps only, no
engine changes.

WHERE IT COMES FROM
  Minibosses! v0.1.1 by Tartlman (ZDoom forum, November 2019; "All code by me. Special thanks to
  the Hideous Destructor server for helping me out!"), on hiatus since 2019 -- the origin of this
  mod: a miniboss with extra lives, thralls tied to it by trails, damage cut per living thrall,
  invulnerability after a lost life and thralls raised again when it ends. Kept unmodified in the
  repo's original/ folder. A rebuild: its code is not copied, except that the thrall markers, the thrall chains
  and the ring of fire are rebuilt to match its effect actors (minibossfx.txt: defaults and states) and its
  controller's placement of them (minibosscontroller.txt) exactly, on the owner's word (2026-11-17), as is its
  damage cut per living thrall (with its integer division fixed). Its to-do list asked for
  "special legendoom compatibility"; this rebuild delivers it.

WHAT THIS PK3 SHIPS, AND WHOSE IT IS
  The project owner (presidentkoopa)
      The set-piece design: thralls as boosted legendary champions, the inherited powers.
      The miniboss fight (2026-11-05): called in, never picked at level load; the meter fed by style, kill chains, Gun
      Bonsai XP and time; the watch check + Use call-in; arrival in view at a distance in slow motion; what
      arrives scaling with progress; the payoff (a gun or power-up, extra bits, the miniboss fight's score row); legendary
      weapons only from miniboss fights with LegenDoom + Champions.
      The fight reworked (2026-11-09, design/MINIBOSS_REWORK.md): the staggered arrival on arrival beams, Boosted
      Legendary Champions, the 1.25x miniboss, the HUD bar stack, "Target!", powers lost with their thrall, no thralls
      rising, Glow In The Dark's part in the fight. (2026-11-17: the immunity, the windows, "Guard!" over the miniboss,
      the grey Xs, the tier-ups and the greed rule went; the original's damage cut and art came back.)
  Joshua Hard (Dynamic Enemy Reinforcements v3, MIT; a fork of AID)
      The idea the miniboss fight's arrival borrows: a map's own monster start spots are places a monster fits, so
      reinforcements are spawned there (as new actors of the map's own types, tested for room, in teleport fog).
      Its code is not copied: bossfight.zs tests its spots with a probe the size of each monster, adds points
      along the player's view, and needs the player's line of sight.
  Tartlman (Minibosses! v0.1.1)
      The effects, exactly as the original draws them (owner 2026-11-17): the IWAD's own sprites in
      AddStencil, the BFG ball over each thrall (BFS1), the chain of plasma balls to it (APLS), their bursts
      (BFE1, APBX) and the ring of fire (FIRE), in one random colour a miniboss as the original's. (Its BAL2
      life orbs are not drawn: the Boss bar's lives stand in for them.)
  Written for this pack
      All other code (zscript/rsqmb), including the pooled VisualThinker drawing of the other bursts. No
      sprites or sounds are shipped: the sprites are the IWAD's, by name, and the cues use the IWAD's own
      sounds by name (weapons/bfgx, imp/shotx, misc/teleport, misc/chat, misc/p_pkup). The arrival's teleport fog (without
      RSQ VisualRewards' beams) is the original's BFE1 / APBX art. Engine particles stand in for those bursts
      only if an IWAD sprite can't be found.
  The HUD (graphics/)
      RSQMBHE / RSQMBHW: coldcite's Top HP Bars bar (via VisualRewards/tools/gen_gauges.py; the same art as RSQ SlowMo's
      RSQSMHE / RSQSMHW). RSQMBSK0 / RSQMBSK1: the crowned skull, original pixel art for this pack. RSQMBWH (a white box)
      made by tools/gen_hudart.py for this pack. RSQMBG01-13 (the tier gems, by RSQ Monsters tier: strength order since 11-11) and RSQMBC00-21 (the
      Champion power icons): the series' own style B icon set (design/icons, 2026-11-10), original art for the series.
