RSQ Gun Cards -- credits
========================

The cards are Ermac's Force Unleashed holo HUD, used with the owner's blessing: the ammo panels
(ammo_bg*.png), the AMMO / MAGAZINE / CHAMBER words and the digits (digit_*.png) in tools/card_art/ are his
art, from his doom-force-unleashed devbuild (models/common). We turn them into voxels; the design is his.
Since 2026-11-03 his panels stay on the small card and his words and digits are its "Small Card Font: Ermac"
option (the default font is JFO, below). graphics/GCEDIG*.png and GCEPLATE.png (the cards drawn in the world)
are made from the same art.

THE TEXT (2026-11-03, the owner's "our font"): JFO, the JDUI font by Zedas, in the edit "JDUI Brutal Doom
Ultimate Edition" by Blackmore1014 (ModDB: https://www.moddb.com/addons/jdui-brutal-doom-ultimate-edition).
tools/card_art/jfo/ holds the glyphs used, copied unchanged from JFO.pk3 (Hires/SmallFont/stcfnNNN.png and
Hires/BigFont/fontbNNN.png); the tools turn them into voxels. JFO ships with credit to Zedas and Blackmore1014, by the owner's
decision (2026-11-04).

THE KEY ICONS on the watch card: BD22_VoxelPack's own key voxels seen from the front (tools/card_art/keys/vp_*.png,
from Voxels/Keys/RKEYA / YKEYA / BKEYA.kvx, keycards by Ghostpilot, and RSKUA / YSKUA / BSKUA.kvx, skull keys by
Voxelbro, per the voxel pack's Credits.txt), shown when the voxel pack is loaded; without it, Freedoom's status-bar
keys (tools/card_art/keys/fd_*.png, STKEYS0-5 from freedoom2.wad, BSD licence).

Code: ZScript written for the Radiant Silvergun Quest series (presidentkoopa), for stock QuestZDoom 17.3 by
Emawind and Team Beef. No engine change. Moved out of RSQ Wheel (the owner's RS_WeaponSelectionSystem port),
whose look reader, Gun Bonsai reader and billboards it carries its own copies of.

The cards in the world (Draw in the World) use the loaded IWAD's DoomFont and status bar by lump name.

Gun Bonsai is ToxicFrog's; the watch card only reads it, through RSQ Gun Bonsai VR's service, when loaded.
