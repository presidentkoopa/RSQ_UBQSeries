## F4: A ledge hooked from far away is not reached, though the hook's range allows the bite
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: core.zs:1008 (gives up after 45 tics), core.zs:1018-1019 (2 units a tic toward the ledge while rising, then at most 12 a tic over), core.zs:555-581 (LedgeSpot has no distance test)
- What the code does: a ledge bite is accepted at any distance up to the range (780 units). The lift then moves you at 2 units a tic sideways while you rise, and at most 12 a tic after that, for at most 45 tics. That covers roughly 480 to 540 units. From farther away it stops in mid air ("ledge.fail") and you fall. A floor bite on any raised floor more than a step above your feet (core.zs:570-576) is treated the same way, so hooking the top of a low platform across a room gives this slow carry, not the shove.
- Why it looks wrong: a wall bite from the same spot shoves you at up to 65 units a tic. The ledge bite from the same spot carries you at 12 and can fail short. The player would see the hook bite a far ledge, drift toward it slowly, then let go.
- Sure?: unsure. The numbers are read from the code; a rig test settles it: stand 600 units from a 64-unit ledge, hook just under its top.
- Fix A: only treat a bite as a ledge when you are within about 400 units of it; farther away, shove as for a wall (2 lines in LedgeSpot).
- Fix B: make the lift's speed follow the distance as the pull to a monster does (distance / 12, at least 12), and drop the 45-tic limit for a "no headway" test (about 8 lines in TickMantle).
- Or intended: the README adds a Limits line: a ledge is reached only from within about 15 m.
