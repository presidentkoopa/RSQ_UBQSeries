RSQ Grapple 2.0 -- credits
==========================

Design: presidentkoopa (design/PLANNED_PROJECTS.md "Grapple 2.0", 2026-11-10 to 11-13): the off hand's hook,
its three results, the kick-chain, the double jump and its time markers, the air spike.

jpalomo: the hookshot this is rebuilt from (jp_grapple.pk3, kept unmodified in jp_original/ of the source repo).
  - its feel: the claw flies as his HookShot does (40 units a tic, his radius 10 and height 14); the wall thrust is his Hook_HitWall (hook.acs), number for number; the reach is his 780 units (about 23 m);
    the fly-to takes his distance / 12 a tic; the claw stays shut his 16 tics after a wall bite
  - its art and sound, shipped under this mod's names: claw sprites OCLW (open) and CCLW (shut) as RSGH / RSGK, the chain
    link tenda0 (the 40 px hires version) as RSGL, and dschain, dspulchn, dsmtalht, dsmeatht as RSGCHAIN, RSGPULCH,
    RSGMETAL, RSGMEAT
Thank you, jpalomo.

The kick's sound and its whoosh (sounds/rsqgr/kick.ogg, whoosh.ogg; 2026-11-16, design/MELEE_SOUNDS.md): made from the
owner's punch7 and punch10 (presidentkoopa's uploads) -- FROM STREETS OF RAGE 2 (Sega, 1992; owner 2026-11-17) -- with RSQ Melee's thud2 (credited
there) under the kick; baked by RSQ Melee's tools/meleesounds.py.

Asked by name from other series packs (none shipped here): RSQ Melee's MLK4 hit burst (kiddolink's "VFX Hit Impact",
the owner's licence) drawn gold for the kick, and its thud; RSQ VisualRewards' words (KICK!, the time markers); RSQ Grenade's
Neon Crescent; RSQ SlowMo's slow motion; RSQ Miniboss's guard and Can't Be Yanked.
The air spike's flames are the IWAD's own (Doom II's FIRE), read from the IWAD, not shipped.

Code: ZScript written for the Radiant Silvergun Quest series (presidentkoopa), for stock QuestZDoom 17.3 by Emawind84
and Team Beef. No engine change. The menu pieces (tools/rsmenu) and the hand sync (tools/handsync) are the series' own.
