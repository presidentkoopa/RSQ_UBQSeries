RSQ Grapple 2.0 -- credits
==========================

Design: presidentkoopa (design/PLANNED_PROJECTS.md "Grapple 2.0", 2026-11-10 to 11-13): the off hand's instant hook,
its three results, the kick-chain, the double jump and its time markers, the air spike, Scorpion Pull.

jpalomo: the hookshot this is rebuilt from (jp_grapple.pk3, kept unmodified in jp_original/ of the source repo).
  - its feel: the wall thrust is his Hook_HitWall (hook.acs), number for number; the reach is his 780 units (about 23 m);
    a yank takes his distance / 12 a tic; the claw stays shut his 16 tics after a wall bite
  - its art and sound, shipped under this mod's names: claw sprites OCLW (open) and CCLW (shut) as RSGH / RSGK, the chain
    link tenda0 (the 40 px hires version) as RSGL, and dschain, dspulchn, dsmtalht, dsmeatht as RSGCHAIN, RSGPULCH,
    RSGMETAL, RSGMEAT
Thank you, jpalomo.

Asked by name from other series packs (none shipped here): RSQ Melee's MLK4 hit burst (kiddolink's "VFX Hit Impact",
the owner's licence) drawn gold for the kick, and its thud; RSQ VisualRewards' words (KICK!, the time markers); RSQ Grenade's
Neon Crescent; RSQ SlowMo's slow motion; RSQ Miniboss's guard and Can't Be Yanked; RSQ Accessibility's Comfort Vignette.
The air spike's flames are the IWAD's own (Doom II's FIRE), read from the IWAD, not shipped.

The "GET OVER HERE" pull is named after a fighting-game move; no sound, voice, art or other asset from Mortal Kombat is used.

Code: ZScript written for the Radiant Silvergun Quest series (presidentkoopa), for stock QuestZDoom 17.3 by Emawind84
and Team Beef. No engine change. The menu pieces (tools/rsmenu) and the hand sync (tools/handsync) are the series' own.
