# Renders

Every render made in the cloud test rig during the 2026-10-27 sessions (the owner asked for all of them in the repo).

- Frames come from the headless QuestZDoom 17.3 rig (software GL, fixed poses), so they show placement and behaviour, not Quest brightness or feel.
- Until 16:00 on 10-27 the rig used Freedoom 2 as its IWAD, so fonts, textures and monsters in those frames are Freedoom's; after that, the owner's doom2.wad.
- `scratch/`: the coordinator's sheets and frames (laser `fE/`, seated mode `seat_frames/`, colour-blind `cb_frames/`, mirror and cigarette sheets).
- `session/`: the agents' frame dumps (`frames/<run>` HP bars and grapple, `f/` colour-blind, `shots/`, `mock/` the shelved first screens attempt, `dump*` and others).
- `agents/`: evidence images committed in the agents' worktrees (`test/evidence/`).
- Identical images are kept once. Raw PPM frame dumps (about 9.5 GB) are not included.
