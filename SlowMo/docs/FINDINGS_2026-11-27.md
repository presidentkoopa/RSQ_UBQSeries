# RSQ SlowMo: findings from reading the code (2026-11-27)

Read at commit 1a846b85c. Every ZScript file SlowMo includes was read whole, with KEYCONF, CVARINFO, MENUDEF, MAPINFO
and the small definition lumps. Nothing was run: no rig, no headset. Each finding is a question for the owner. Nothing
was changed.

Paths are inside `SlowMo/` unless they start with another folder. `clock.zs`, `down.zs` and the other `.zs` files are in
`zscript/rsqsm/`. "Old README" is `SlowMo/README.md` as it stood at that commit.

Ordered by what a player would meet first. Wording and comment mismatches are at the end.

## F1: Held slow motion ends without a sound or rumble when the adrenaline meter runs out
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: clock.zs:1727 (the meter at zero calls `Aim(false, -1)`), clock.zs:639-644 (the way-out sound and rumble need a player number); compare clock.zs:1230 (Slow Motion Length ends the hold with `heldWho`, so that end has its cue)
- What the code does: with Adrenaline on (the default) the usual end of your held slow motion is the meter reaching zero. That end passes player -1, so the lift sound and both hands' rumble are skipped. Pressing the key, crossing the wrists, the Wheel closing and Slow Motion Length all pass a real player and do play them.
- Why it looks wrong: `heldWho` is stored "for his cue when it lets go" (clock.zs:75) and is not used here. The player gets no signal that time is about to come back, on the one ending he does not choose.
- Sure?: certain about the code path. Whether the silence is wanted is the owner's call.
- Fix A: pass `heldWho` at clock.zs:1727 (1 line, 1 file).
- Fix B: a different cue for "ran dry" (the low buzz and "oof" the refusal uses, clock.zs:620-621), so it reads apart from letting go yourself (about 4 lines, 1 file).
- Or intended: the README keeps its row: at zero slow motion ends, with no sound or rumble.

## F2: A refused Crisis Dive says nothing
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: clock.zs:1052-1056 (refused in the air, ducked, in water, or where jumping is off: a debug log line only); compare clock.zs:1057-1063 (refused for adrenaline: a buzz and the "oof")
- What the code does: the dive needs you on the ground, your crouch factor at 0.75 or more, water below waist level and jumping allowed on the map. If any fails the key does nothing at all. Only the adrenaline refusal is felt and heard.
- Why it looks wrong: the crouch factor is your real head height when QuestZDoom's crouch is head tracked (down.zs:501-502 describes that mode). A player who is ducking, short, or seated can be under 0.75 and the key will seem dead with no hint why.
- Sure?: certain about the code. Unsure how often a standing player reads under 0.75 on the headset: a headset test with the debug log on (`rsqsm_debug 1`) would settle it.
- Fix A: play the refusal buzz and "oof" on these refusals too (2 lines, 1 file).
- Fix B: drop the crouch test, so a ducked or seated player can dive (1 line, 1 file).
- Fix C: both.
- Or intended: the README keeps its row: in the air, ducked, in deep water or on a no-jump map the key does nothing, with no cue.

## F3: With a saber or the ShieldSaw in hand, RSQ Score's big moment is read as Force Speed or a block
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: clock.zs:445-450 (any pulse at 35% that is not 500 ms goes to the saber or shield first), clock.zs:465-466 (Score and Gun Bonsai VR are tested only after); Score/zscript/rsscore/handler.zs:698-699 (Score's big moment is 600 ms at 35%); clock.zs:579 (Gun Bonsai VR's Adrenaline at level 5 works out to 1050 ms at 35%)
- What the code does: a pulse of 600 ms at 35% from a player holding a saber is let in as `saber.forcespeed`: deliberate, no switch, no cooldown. Holding the ShieldSaw with its Perfect-Block Slow-Mo on, it is let in as `shieldsaw.block`. It is never tested as Score's. The same happens to Gun Bonsai VR's Adrenaline pulse at level 5.
- Why it looks wrong: the comment at clock.zs:463 says "Score's and Gun Bonsai VR's pulses first". With a saber out, the Score row (clock.zs:674) no longer switches Score's big moment off, the Auto Cooldown no longer spaces it, and `trigger.score` answers 0 (clock.zs:2484), so Score's Bullet Time bonus is paid for Score's own pulse (Score/zscript/rsscore/physical.zs:710 skips it only on 1).
- Sure?: certain about the order of the tests. Not run: the debug log (`rsqsm_debug 1`) on a big moment with a saber drawn would show `trigger saber.forcespeed`.
- Fix A: move the two lines at clock.zs:465-466 above the block at clock.zs:445 (2 lines moved, 1 file).
- Fix B: Score and Gun Bonsai VR send a marker event with their pulse, as Melee, the Rig, Grenade and the Saber's remote do, and SlowMo reads it (about 6 lines here, 2 lines in each sender; 3 mods).
- Or intended: the README's Works with row for RSQ Score would add: with a saber or ShieldSaw in hand its big moment counts as that weapon's own slow motion.

## F4: The weapon sets' Quick Grenade and Skeet Window are not recognised
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: clock.zs:384-385 (only `rsqgn_skeetslowmo` and `rsqgn_quickslowmo` are read); weaponsets/GNRC_Reloaded/zscript/rsqb/gn_thrown.zs:213 and :226, weaponsets/WoS_Reloaded/zscript/rsqb/gn_thrown.zs:213 and :226 (they send `rsqb_gn_quickslowmo` and `rsqb_gn_skeetslowmo`)
- What the code does: the grenade copies in GNRC Reloaded and WoS Reloaded name their pulses with their own prefix. SlowMo does not know those names, so their pulses fall through to Gun Bonsai VR's test (a Skeet Window of 400, 550 or 700 ms at World Speed matches Time Dilation, clock.zs:582) or to Other Mods (clock.zs:468): automatic, under the wrong row, and refused during the Auto Cooldown.
- Why it looks wrong: the comment at clock.zs:434-435 says this exact mix up was fixed for RSQ Grenade. A quick throw from a weapon set's grenade gets no slow motion for 25 seconds after any automatic one, and the Quick Grenade row does not govern it.
- Sure?: certain about the names. Not run with either weapon set.
- Fix A: read the two `rsqb_gn_` names beside the `rsqgn_` ones at clock.zs:384-385 (2 lines, 1 file).
- Fix B: the weapon sets send the `rsqgn_` names (2 lines in each of 2 files, outside SlowMo).
- Or intended: the README's Works with table would add a row: GNRC Reloaded and WoS Reloaded grenades count as Other Mods.

## F5: The game's own slow motion starts and ends with no sound
- Kind: README claimed, not in code
- Where: old README line 75 ("You hear Doom's power-up pickup as the world slows and a lift landing as it comes back, with a rumble in both hands"); clock.zs:713-758 (Trigger, the way in for every pulse, plays nothing); clock.zs:641-643, :905-907, :1086-1088 (the only cues: the held toggle, the burst, the dive)
- What the code does: the in and out sounds and the rumble play only when you turn the hold on or off, start a burst or start a dive. A pulse (a heavy hit, a barrel, the cigarette, a parry, any other mod's) changes the world's speed with no sound or rumble from SlowMo. Slowed things' sounds drop in pitch, and the countdown bar shows.
- Why it looks wrong: the old README reads as if every slow motion has the cue.
- Sure?: certain.
- Fix A: none in code; the new README already says which starts have a cue.
- Fix B: play the in sound when a pulse is let in from full speed, and the out sound when the clock is back at 1 (about 8 lines, 1 file).
- Or intended: as the new README has it.

## F6: Without RSQ Score the DOWN count never goes back
- Kind: other
- Where: down.zs:425 (no Score service: the lives watch returns before the reset at down.zs:432), down.zs:731 (each DOWN adds one), down.zs:218 and :1407 (third DOWN on: a 3 second clock and 33% health); the header at down.zs:27-28 says "without Score nothing resets it"
- What the code does: the count lives on the player (RSQSM_DownWatch) and travels through every map. Only a Score life being spent sets it to 0. Playing without RSQ Score, the third DOWN and every one after it, for the rest of the game, starts at 3 seconds and gets you up at a third of your health.
- Why it looks wrong: WHO_OWNS_WHAT says no mod requires another. Here the standalone game gets steadily harder with no way back.
- Sure?: certain about the code. Unsure whether a death and level restart hands back the count from the level's start: a rig test would settle it.
- Fix A: without Score, set the count to 0 at each new map (about 4 lines in down.zs WorldLoaded, 1 file).
- Fix B: without Score, set it to 0 after a set time without going DOWN (about 8 lines and one setting, 2 files).
- Or intended: the README keeps its limit: without RSQ Score the count is never set back.
