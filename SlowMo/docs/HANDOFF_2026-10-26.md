## 2026-10-30, branch `slowmo-fixes` -- three fixes from the engine survey (apply/05_SLOWMO)

The survey pass over this mod against qzd17.3 found three bugs and otherwise confirmed the
mechanism: the per-actor clock is the right choice and freezetics would be a regression (it
strobes at ~8.75 Hz and cannot ease, so it would delete the ramps and the Min SlowMo Ease
floor). Nothing was restructured. Not compiled and not played -- the first real check is the
headset. Engine line numbers below were re-verified in the qzd17.3 tree.

- **Impulses() predicted a tic of gravity for a FROZEN actor.** `AActor::Tick` returns on
  `freezetics` (`p_mobj.cpp:3948-3952`) before gravity is applied, so the prediction came out
  a whole gravity step low and the remainder was read as a scripted push: -(1-f) of it was
  written back as velocity every tic (0.75 units/tic at depth 0.25, past the 0.5 gate),
  invisible while frozen and a kick downward the moment it unfroze. Reachable by picking up a
  vanilla time sphere while slow motion runs -- a time freezer is a different mechanic and is
  rightly not in the rival list. Now an early `if (a.isFrozen()) return;` (`actor.zs:513`,
  which short-circuits on `freezetics` at `actorinlines.h:174-177`, so every freeze route is
  covered), and the ground-slide compensation in `Process` is skipped for a frozen thing too.
- **The friction prediction guarded bMISSILE only.** `P_XYMovement` skips friction for
  `MF_MISSILE`, `MF_SKULLFLY` and `MF8_NOFRICTION` alike (`p_mobj.cpp:2442-2445`), so a
  charging Lost Soul, anything mid-`A_SkullAttack` and any `+NOFRICTION` mod actor at floor
  level were compensated for a friction they never paid, and then "corrected" by Impulses (at
  ORIG_FRICTION and a 20 units/tic charge the discrepancy is ~1.9 units, well past the gate):
  net, a charge bled speed in slow motion. Both sites now carry `!bSKULLFLY && !bNOFRICTION`.
- **Repitch never touched channel 0, so no CHAN_AUTO sound was ever repitched.** `CHAN_AUTO` is
  channel 0 (`base.zs:6`) and a sound started on it stays there -- the engine sets
  `CHANF_OVERLAP` and lets them overlap on 0 rather than hijacking a slot
  (`s_sound.cpp:536-541`). The loop started at 1. It starts at 0 now. The two natives read 0
  differently and both are safe: `A_SoundPitch(0)` retunes channel 0 only
  (`ChangeSoundPitch` matches `chan->EntChannel == channel`, `s_sound.cpp:1057-1068`), while
  `IsActorPlayingSound(0)` answers "playing anything, on any channel"
  (`IsSourcePlayingSomething`, `s_sound.cpp:1151-1164`) -- a superset, so the guard can never
  skip a channel-0 sound and at worst lets through one A_SoundPitch that finds nothing.

Not done from that survey, by scope: the walking strobe (~2.9 Hz at depth 0.25; a puppeted-step
controller at `STAT_BOT`, cost M, and worth looking at in the headset at 0.25 / 0.4 / 0.5
before spending it), a `freezetics` tier for the far field, the GLDEFS post-process grade, and
`VisualThinker` ejecta. The weapon wheel cancelling a player-held slow motion on close is real
and is a fix in the Wheel, not here: it can ask `RSQSM_Service` "active" on open and skip the
`rsm_off` on close.

## 2026-10-27, branch `agent-polish` (independent review item 5) -- read first

Not yet run on the owner's headset. Rig B (QZD 17.3 Linux, doom2 MAP01), probe `pol/smprobe` in the session
scratchpad (N invisible ticking actors spread round the player, `netevent rsm_on`), Gather timed inside
the clock by an instrumented copy (MSTimeF round the call; not shipped).

- **Gather no longer searches the list for every actor.** `act.Find` per actor (Consider and Gather) was
  n^2/2 compares. The list is in the iterator's order (first look in walk order, spawns in first-tick
  order = the order the engine appends them, Drop keeps the order), so Gather walks `act` alongside the
  iterator: one compare per listed actor, a full search only for one out of step (it came into range
  later), and the walk jumps to where it was found. Exact by construction. The first look searches nothing.
  Consider still searches for a spawn (one search per spawn, the double-listing guard of 026b277).

  | actors listed | first look before -> after | each later look (17 tics) before -> after | no membership test at all (floor) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1543 | 2.34 -> 2.19 ms | 1.05 -> 0.84 ms | 0.68 ms |
  | 3543 | 8.05 -> 6.03 ms | 4.02 -> 2.35 ms | 2.39 ms |
  | 4000 (cap) | 13.83 -> 7.72 ms | 6.26 -> 3.05 ms | -- |

  The rest of a look is the iterator plus the range and real-time tests per actor (~0.6 us each).
- **A Map was measured first and rejected**: ZScript maps take only int / String keys and an Actor has no
  number (SpawnOrder is not exposed in 17.3), so the key is `String.Format("%p", a)`; that cost as much as the
  search (later look at 3543: 4.00 ms map vs 3.91 search, same build otherwise).
- Listed counts identical before and after (1543 / 3543 / 4000), no script error.

## Done (measured on the real qzd17.3 engine, tools/rig17, freedoom2 MAP01)

- **Clock.** `netevent rsm_on`, depth 0.25: an imp's walk states 3 -> 12 tics, its
  ground speed ~1/4 (500 -> 365 units in 236 tics vs 500 -> 172 in ~140); DoomImpBall
  10 -> 2.5 units/tic. `rsm_off`: states back to 3 tics, fireballs 10. `rsm_pulse 1000 30`
  holds 0.30 for 35 tics then eases out; the arrays empty when it is over.
- **Rounds.** A zombieman's hitscan in slow motion -> RSQSM_Round at 14 units/tic,
  hitting the player for the shot's damage (3-15), credited to the Zombieman with the
  round as inflictor. A 40-unit sidestep 3 tics after the shot: missed in 3 of 3 runs
  (passed 37, 44, 62 units from the head). Frame dump: the tracer streak. Before the
  hit was dealt in SpecialMissileHit, a round that met another zombieman did NOT hurt
  him (the engine's projectile species rule); the fix deals it like a hitscan.
  Re-measured: two zombiemen in a line, the far one's rounds hit the near one 5 times
  (3, 12, 12, 9, 6), credited to the shooter.
- **Brutal Doom.** BD22's ZombieManTracer (a speed-50 projectile) slows to 12.5 units/tic.
  Reload moment: BD22 Rifle magazine 20 -> 31, reserve 30 -> 19 -> the pulse fires
  (BD reaches its reload through tokens, so the Reload-state check alone saw nothing).
- **Triggers.** Wrist-cross gesture held 25 tics -> ON. Adrenaline on: empty meter
  refuses `rsm_on`; 102 damage taken -> 51; ON drains 35 in 120 tics (10 s per meter).
- **Load order.** brutal22test6 + BD_VoxelPack + the series + RSQ_TacticalRig: no script
  errors beyond BD_VoxelPack's own (identical without the series).

## Review fixes (independent cold review, 2026-10-26)

- A spawned thing is scaled in full at its spawn event, which comes just before its
  first tic, after its velocity is set (the old "fresh" guess let missiles spawned during
  the ramp-out keep up to 25% extra speed for good). Rounds leave the muzzle already slowed.
  Re-measured: DoomImpBall spawns at 2.5; rounds at 14 from the first tic.
- Gravity is scaled whatever NOGRAVITY says (dying clears it), and a script's own change
  to gravity or a missile's speed is taken as the new base.
- Test traces (no damage, NOINTERACT) and absolute-position attacks are not converted;
  only shooters inside the slowed range fire rounds. Rounds pass non-shootable things
  like a hitscan, carry the puff's damage type and its THRUGHOST / SPECTRAL / FOILINVUL /
  PAINLESS / FORCEPAIN, and do not trigger projectile-cross lines.
- Synced clock state is no longer wiped on load; the adrenaline meter is carried on the
  player (`RSQSM_AdrToken`) so "keep it between levels" works; leaving a level restores
  every slowed thing; things already in the world are gathered only in range, and things
  that come into range are added every 17 tics; settings read once a tic.
