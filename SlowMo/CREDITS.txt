RSQ_SlowMo -- credits
=====================

The world slows, you don't. Part of the Radiant Silvergun Quest series. For stock QuestZDoom 17.3:
ZScript and lumps only, no engine changes. The code is new, written for this pack.

WHAT THIS PK3 SHIPS, AND WHOSE IT IS
  The project owner (presidentkoopa)
      The design: RS_SlowMo's (made for his engine build): its events (rsm_pulse, rsm_toggle,
      rsm_on, rsm_off, rsm_state), its settings and the rule of standing down for another
      slow-motion mod.
      The round's tracer mesh (models/rsqsm/btracer.obj, btracer.png) and the near-miss whiz
      and crack recordings (sounds/rsqsm/smwhiz1-4, smcrak1-2): his RS_Ballistics library.
  Generated for this pack
      The round's placeholder sprite (sprites/rsqsm/RSQRA0.png).
      DOWN's sounds (sounds/rsqsm/smdnhit, smdnring, smdnbeat, smdnrlf): synthesized from sine waves and seeded noise by
      tools/down_audio/gen_down_sounds.py, no samples (2026-11-09).
  coldcite (Top HP Bars 1.04, 2017: "Feel free to use this in your mods. You don't have to ask my permission!")
      The slow-motion countdown bar (graphics/RSQSMHE empty, RSQSMHW the fill in white that the HUD tints) is his
      style-A health bar left lying as he drew it, with the series' bar rim and well (VisualRewards/tools/gen_gauges.py in the
      series repo).
  THE DOWN PLATE'S TEXT (owner 2026-11-07: RSQ Score's combo font when Score is loaded, else JFO)
      graphics/rsqsmp/RSPCnnn.png: the letters of RSQ Score's breakdown (combo) font, RSSCFN2, unpacked from
      Score/RSSCFN2.fon2 by tools/plate/gen_plate_font.py. RSQ Score carries it from ijon's Dakka, whose credits give the
      breakdown font to Jimmy (Score/CREDITS.md: "They come with Dakka, a public repository, and are carried here with
      that credit").

      Blackmore1014 (ModDB: https://www.moddb.com/addons/jdui-brutal-doom-ultimate-edition), the same glyph files RSQ Gun

      Hires/SmallFont/stcfnNNN.png) by tools/plate/gen_plate_font.py. JFO ships with credit to Zedas and Blackmore1014,
      by the owner's decision (2026-11-04). Characters JFO does not have (. , ( ) ' ! ? % and the like) are the IWAD's own

  Kirby Mass Attack (HAL Laboratory / Nintendo), per the owner (2026-11-10)

      Attack's, supplied by the owner.
  id Software (Doom), played by name from the IWAD, NOT shipped
