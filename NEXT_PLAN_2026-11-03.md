## The next three jobs (owner, 11-03 15:50)

### 1. Merge Top HP Bars, the damage numbers and Kill Rewards into one mod, under a new name

Today the three live in two places:
- **Top HP Bars** (`TopHPBars/`, `pk3/RSQ_HPBars.pk3`): coldcite's bars, plus the damage numbers (Xaser Acheron's
  damnums, MIT, folded in on 11-03 and rebuilt on VisualThinkers: JFO digits, ramp, running total, OVERKILL,
  HEADSHOT, gains to the wrist).
- **Kill Rewards** (inside `GunBonsaiOffhandVR/rsq/zscript/killrewards.zs`, `rsqgb_kr_*`): SoM's Universal Kill
  Rewards, with the owner's defaults (By Health fountain, 50 % per bit, 15/20/65 mix, any gun, 7 s, boss x3),
  the BD helper blacklist, the miniboss "Boss Kill Rewards" hook, and Gun Bonsai VR's path nodes (Bit
  Scavenger, Rich Bits, Magnet, Lucky Kill) reading it.

The plan:
1. **Pick the name** (owner; options below). The folder, the pk3 and the menu title take it; class and cvar
   prefixes stay as they are where renaming would break saved settings (or migrate once, the way the Wheel's
   config generations do).
2. **Move Kill Rewards out of Gun Bonsai VR into the new mod**: the handler, the three bit classes and their
   sprites (KRBH/KRBA/KRBM), the cvars, the menu page, CREDITS (SoM). Keep the cvar names (`rsqgb_kr_*`) or
   migrate them once, so the owner's settings survive.
3. **Gun Bonsai VR keeps the path nodes** and asks the new mod by name or service (a small service: "bits
   per kill", "rich", "magnet", "lucky"), so Gun Bonsai VR still works alone and the new mod still works alone
   (at level 0 it is SoM's behaviour, as today). The miniboss hook (`RSQMB_Service` "bossfor rewards") moves
   with Kill Rewards.
4. **One shared list of Brutal Doom helpers** (HeadHitbox family, BootSmearers, NewCybersHitBox, the dying
   bodies the owner may add) used by the bars, the numbers and the bits, instead of two copies.
5. **One menu** for the new mod: Bars / Numbers / Rewards pages (this lands after the owner's menu redesign,
   in its style).
6. **One README and CREDITS** (coldcite, Xaser Acheron MIT, SoM, JDUI/Zedas and Blackmore1014 for the JFO
   digits with the licence note).
7. **Proof**: everything the three did before still does it (rig frames and logs), zero script errors on doom2
   alone, with Gun Bonsai + all RSQ pk3s, and on the full order; Gun Bonsai VR's nodes still raise the bits;
   a save from before the merge loads with the owner's settings kept.
8. **Delivery**: the old `RSQ_HPBars.pk3` leaves the load order; Gun Bonsai VR's pk3 loses Kill Rewards.

Name options (the owner picks or names it):
- **RSQ Spoils** — what a kill gives you: bars to read, numbers to see, bits to grab.
- **RSQ Payoff** — every hit and kill pays back on screen and in loot.
- **RSQ Bounty** — the price on every monster's head, shown and paid.
- **RSQ Carnage Readout** — the health, the hits, the haul.
- **RSQ Hit & Haul** — what you hit, what you haul.
