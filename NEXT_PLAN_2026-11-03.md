## Miniboss called-in fights (owner, BEFORE 1.0; decided 11-05 01:28)

Design so far (owner, 11-03): the whole fight (miniboss + thralls) teleports in near the player under conditions,
instead of being picked from the map's own monsters. Example mod the owner gave: Dynamic Enemy Reinforcements v3
(attachment 29f0b2a5-Gameplay_DynamicEnemyReinforcements_v3.pk3) for the teleport-in system; Fading Corpses
(496c55cc-Gameplay_FadingCorpses.pk3) for actor bloat. Triggers: player level-up, Score milestones (owner open to
more ideas). All monster behaviour stays normal. Arrival fog/art from Miniboss's own (original Minibosses!) sprites.
Needs room for the fight (a certain size room). Owner: no locked decisions on Minibosses without a discussion:
settle the open details with him before building.
Settled 11-05 01:3x: CALLED-IN ONLY (no miniboss picked at level load); what arrives SCALES WITH PROGRESS (stronger
types as the player's level/score grows); arrival IN VIEW, AT A DISTANCE (the player watches them teleport in).
NAME: "Bossfight" (owner 11-05: not "Heat"). The Bossfight meter sits under the SCORE word, above the SlowMo countdown
bar, above the combos.
TRIGGER (owner 11-05 01:3x): the meter + YOU CALL IT. The meter fills from Score's style bonuses/combos (most), kill
chains, Gun Bonsai XP, slow time pushing into the map (without Score: kills + time). Full = telegraphed (Glow tint +
heartbeat, sound sting, "HEAT FULL" on Wheel/Gun Cards). The player calls it in with the watch check + Use, or it
arrives by itself after ~60 s. Arrival in view at a distance, slow-motion landing. Tier from Gun Bonsai player level +
recent Score; types already in the map. Guardrails: not during a heavy fight or on low health, needs a big enough room
(waits), never in the first minute or at the exit, cooldown + per-map cap. Payoff for a KILL (owner 11-05): a guaranteed weapon or power-up drop, tons of Kill Rewards bits, Champions' normal
champion loot, Score's rows; the greed rule stays. LEGENDARY WEAPONS: when LegenDoom AND Champions are both loaded,
Bossfights are the only source of legendary weapon drops (other legendaries stay Champions' boosted dormant champions
with their extra bits and loot); LegenDoom alone is unaffected (normal LegenDoom).
