- `RS_Retier.zs`: `Request` hands a kit monster to `RequestKit` (the same flash, then the kit moves; `kitRung` on the

  `rs_bdtier_<colour>` translations (my values); MAPINFO handler.

  never calls in turn; a patch of the same kind within 40 units is renewed, not stacked; "set" 2.
