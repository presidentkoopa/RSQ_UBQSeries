## Bugs
1. **Whip and Gun's stale gold diamond (N2), fixed** (separate commit): `Whip/zscript/rsqwp/swing.zs` DrawAim returned
   early on a red aim without removing the ring and centre drawn by an earlier gold aim. Shown fixed on the rig.
2. **QuestZDoom's (M8F) choice with BD22 VR loaded (L2), not fixed:** no M8F dot, and a muzzle flash and tracer every frame (BD22 VR's
   player-hitscan hooks take M8F's per-tic LineAttack). The fix is outside RSQ Laser Sight (BD22 VR, or the menu). **Owner decides.**
3. **Range readout not visible (L5), not fixed:** the value is right in the log, but no readout is drawn in either placement.
   Suspect: the RSLRANGE canvas texture in VR (`LaserSight/zscript/rsqls/sight.zs`, UiTick and UpdateReadout). The fix
   needs a design choice (for example billboard glyphs, as Score's wrist digits use). **Owner decides.**
