Doom Arcade VR: the Miniboss fight, beat by beat

Seven mockups, one per beat of the fight as decided (design/MINIBOSS_REWORK.md, "The fight, as decided"), made the way the arcade-language panels are: real rig frames (QuestZDoom 17.3, left eye, Doom II MAP02, the full load order) with the HUD and the words drawn where they would sit, in the series' own fonts and HUD art. Nothing of the rework is built: these are pictures. Click a panel for full size.

What is real, what is drawn, what is placeholder

  • Real (in the rig frames): the room, the 1.25x MeatGrinder baron (scale and hitbox) and the ColorfulHell imps, the teleport fogs, RSQ Monsters' own tier flash and colour changes (panels 4 and 6), RSQ Glow In The Dark's own events (the arrival wave, the red and blue room glows in panels 5 and 6), the deaths and gore, the loot (a megasphere and a soulsphere spawned for the shot), RSQ VisualRewards' Kill Rewards bits.
  • Drawn with the series' own art: every bar (coldcite's Top HP Bars art as RSQ SlowMo's bar, RSQSMHE/W, gen_gauges.py), the Bossfight skull (RSQMBSK1), SlowMo's countdown bar and hourglass (panel 3), DOWN's reward bar as the window (redrawn from downhud.zs's numbers), the words in RS Score's fonts (RSSCFON; the rows in RSSCFN2), the grey X (the damage-number X, VisualRewards dn_art, JFO small).
  • Placeholder art: the silver shield icon (the series has no shield art yet; drawn on the skull's 16 x 16 grid); the sphere pop of a thrall's power (panel 3, an orange burst).
  • Graded in the mockup (not what the rig showed): the cold grey room glow (panel 2: the rig's Glow In The Dark tint read olive on this room's brown walls) and the gold glow and slow-motion desaturation (panel 3).
  • Example values: the points in panel 7 (+4000 Miniboss, +5000 Bossfight). Champions were off in this run, so the Boosted Legendary Champions' own colours and icons are not shown.
  • The tier flash's white (rs_tier_flashwhite) reads grey in this room's light (panels 4 and 6).
