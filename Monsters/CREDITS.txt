RSQ Monsters -- credits

COLOURFUL HELL
  By Hege Cactus (ZDoom forums: "Colourful Hell", https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?t=47980).
  Every ColorfulHell monster here -- its colours, names, numbers, attacks, sounds and option switches -- is Hege Cactus'
  Colourful Hell, rebuilt in ZScript from its DECORATE for stock QuestZDoom 17.3. The code also follows its CHP
  (Colourful Hell Plus) edition in places; that edition's author is still to be credited here.
  Its sprites: the ones this pack's monsters draw. 670 of them (49 sprite sets our monsters named but the pack never
  carried, among them the Abyss Imp, the Abyss Cacodemon, Mr Bones, the Moloch Wraith, the Abyss Arch-vile's tentacles
  and portals, the Black Knight's weapons and the Abyss conversion) were added 2026-11-16 from Colourful Hell 1.04
  (Enemies_ColourfulHell_1_04.pk3, sprites/monsters/ch104/). Where a Colourful Hell sprite had the same name as one in
  Brutal Doom 22, XMVE, Legion of Bones or Champions and a different picture, ours was renamed (Monsters/docs/
  SPRITE_CLASHES_2026-11-16.md), so both packs draw their own.
