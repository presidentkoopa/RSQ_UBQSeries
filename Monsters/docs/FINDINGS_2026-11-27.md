## F1: When the Monster Tier is set above Red, should every monster stop at Red, or should White be reachable everywhere?

Rig: `rsq_monster_set 3`, `rsq_mon_worldtier 13`, a new MAP01: before 18 White bosses (+24 Special Imps), after 18 Red (design/rigcheck_2026-11-27/logs/MN_F1_before.log:70-78, MN_F1.log:70-77).
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: RS_Retier.zs:1045 (the tier read while a map loads: capped at 40) against RS_BDTier.zs:682-683 (the kits and the follow: capped at 10) and :753 (the map's handler answers -1 until the map is set up); RS_Roster.zs:373-381 (a capped family's spawn raised by that number); MENUDEF.txt:207-219 (names Common to Red only)
- What the code does: the setting itself can hold more than 10 (the console, or RSQ Miniboss: with its Rank Difficulty or Rank Ceiling off a Boss win steps it up to 13, Miniboss/zscript/rsqmb/rank.zs:130). While a new map loads, RS_Retier.WorldTier reads the setting up to 40, so with Monster Set ColorfulHell every map monster is raised to that colour: at 13 they all come as White bosses. After the map is set up everything reads it capped at Red, so kits stop at Red, and later spawns stop at Red.
- Why it looks wrong: a ColorfulHell map started at Monster Tier 11 to 13 is all Abyss, Black or White bosses at once, while the menu shows the value with no name and the kits show Red.
- Sure?: likely. Certain about the code; a rig run (ColorfulHell set, `rsq_mon_worldtier 13`, a new map) settles it.
- Fix A (Recommended): cap it at Red there too: `clamp(w.GetInt(), 1, 10)` at RS_Retier.zs:1045, the same top as RS_BDTier.zs:682 (1 line).
- Fix B: let the Monster Tier reach White everywhere: WORLD_TOP 13 and Abyss, Black, White on the menu (2 lines, 3 menu values).
- Or intended: the README's Limits say a tier above Red makes ColorfulHell maps start as bosses.
