# ---- parts, by the pixel's nearest Doom palette index (paint.py). Anything not listed keeps its own pixels. ----
# skin: the Baron's pink hide is the pinks and reds 16-47 (his upper body, face, horns' roots), his furred legs are the
# browns 48-79 and their darkest browns 1-2, 13-15, 236-239; the few olives 128-159 are leg shading. All one body.
# His horns are drawn in the same browns/pinks (no gray of their own), so they take the skin's palette.
part skin 1:2 13:15 16:79 128:159 236:239
# horn: the grays (claws, teeth).
part horn 3:4 80:111
# kept, by not being listed (the owner's rule 11-13 09:49): 0, 5-8 outlines; greens 9-12, 112-127: the Baron's green
# blood and the green fire in his hands; dark reds 168-191: the Cyberbaron's red lights; yellows, oranges (fire), blues
# (ice).
# NOTE the Baron's skin IS the reds 16-47, so a wound's red is painted with it: his blood is green, his red is skin.

# ---- group rules (applied in this order) ----
group * absorb 0:0,5:8 skin 5
group * keepnear 168:191 1 horn
# The Cyberbaron's arm, leg and gun are metal: every gray on his sprites is metal, not horn (metal keeps its own).
group CBOS,CB4R,CY8D,CB4F,P19D,FBCY keep 3:4 80:111
# Frozen bodies are under ice: grays and blues are ice.
group FZD7,BFZD,FZG1,FZG2,FBCY keep 80:111 3:4
