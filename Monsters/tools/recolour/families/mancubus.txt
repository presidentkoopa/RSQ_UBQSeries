# no horn part: the Mancubus has no spikes or claws. Kept, by not being listed (the owner's rule 11-13 09:49): the arm

# ---- group rules ----
# near-black pixels inside the body are its deepest skin shadows (as the Imp)
group * absorb 0:0,5:8 skin 5
# The arm cannons are rusty: brown pixels inside the gray metal. A skin pixel where a quarter or more of the pixels
# within 3 are metal grays is the gun's rust and keeps its own colour (two passes, so the rust inside the gun joins).
group * keepmajor 3:4,80:111 3 0.25 skin 2
# The fatality (FATF) has a marine in it: his helmet and boots are the tans and olives 128-159 (the Mancubus's own
# hide in these frames is the browns 48-79), his armour the greens (kept). As the Imp's fatality.
group FATF keep 128:159
# The attack frame (FATT H) holds the cannons' muzzle flash: its soft glow is half-transparent pixels over the body;
# they keep their own (else the glow takes the body's colour). The body lit orange by the flash keeps that light (the
# oranges and yellows are fire's: as the Imp's fireball hand).
group FATTH keepalpha 250
# The frozen Mancubus is under ice: its grays are the ice's highlights.
