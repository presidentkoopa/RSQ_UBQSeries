# ---- parts, by the pixel's nearest Doom palette index (paint.py). Anything not listed keeps its own pixels. ----
# skin: the Imp's hide is the brown ramp 64-79 (70% of its pixels), with its darkest browns 1-2, 13-15, 151, 236-239
# and its few light tans 48-63 and 128-159 (face and highlights).
part skin 1:2 13:15 48:79 128:159 236:239
# horn: spikes, claws and teeth are the gray ramp 80-111 with its gray 3 and white 4 tips.
part horn 3:4 80:111
# kept, by not being listed (the owner's rule 11-13 09:49: guns, metal, eyes, blood and fire keep their own):
#   0, 5-8 the near-black outlines; 9-12, 112-127 greens (a marine); 16-47, 168-191 reds and pinks: eyes, mouth,
#   blood and guts (the Imp's blood keeps its red); 160-167, 208-235 yellows and oranges: fire (TRO1 holds a fireball,
#   and the hand beside it is lit orange: that light is the fire's); 192-207, 240-255 blues and the rest (ice).

# ---- group rules: where the palette ranges overlap (applied in this order) ----
# (* = every lump) The source's near-black pixels inside the body are its deepest skin shadows, not outlines: on a
# White or Gray imp they would show as dirt. A near-black pixel with 5 or more skin neighbours is skin.
group * absorb 0:0,5:8 skin 5
# Grays touching red are teeth in a mouth, or bone in a wound: they keep their own white (a Yellow imp's red spikes
# must not give it red teeth or red bones).
group * keepnear 16:47,168:191 1 horn
# The fatality (IPFP) has a marine in it: his helmet and boots are the tans 128-159, his armour the greens; his
# gun and belt are grays touching him.
group IPFP keep 128:159
group IPFP keepnear 112:159 2 horn
# The frozen imp is under ice: the grays are the ice's highlights, not spikes.
group IFZD,FZD4 keep 80:111 3:4

# ---- lumps that keep the old flat tint (paint.py does not know their parts) ----
# X728 is XMVE's Dark Imp grabbing a marine (black body, its own look): it keeps what it had.
tint X728
# BOSBA0-E0 are BD22's Hell Knight art (sprites/Monsters/BruiserBrothers/HellKnight, the Knight burnt): the Hell Knight
# family's (hellknight.txt) since 11-13; only BOSBF0 is an imp gib. Not baked here, so this bake never overwrites them.
skip BOSBA,BOSBB,BOSBC,BOSBD,BOSBE

# ---- hand-written masks (rectangles in the lump's pixels) ----
masks imp_masks.txt
