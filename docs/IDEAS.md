# Miniboss ideas (not built -- feature freeze until 1.0 is built and headset-tested)

From the owner and the coordinator, 10-27.

- **Thrall orbs.** Each thrall gets its own orb around the miniboss, in that thrall's colour/power; it blinks
  out when the thrall dies, so you see at a glance which powers still feed the boss. (The original mod had
  orbs that blinked out per thrall; the owner meant to work them into the puzzle but never built it.)
- **Orbs as a puzzle.**
  - Order matters: killing the thrall whose power the boss is using right now gives a longer slow window.
  - Orbs as targets: strike or shoot an orb during a slow window to stun the boss or drop its shield for a moment.
  - Revive clock: a dead thrall's orb flickers back as it is about to rise, so you know how long you have.
- **Powers stay** until the miniboss dies (owner's choice over stripping them).
- **EX tier** (RS Monsters) reached through the miniboss / boosted legendary champion set piece -- see
  /home/claude/work/RSMONSTERS_INVESTIGATION.md.
- Open questions from 1.0: lives (3 total vs 3 extra), greed trigger before any thrall dies, infighting kills
  opening the window, a floor for early maps, exact transfer of controller-driven Champions powers, revived
  thralls' health.
