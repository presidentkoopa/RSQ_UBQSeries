## 2026-10-27, branch `agent-laser` (latest; read this first)

The beam is a psprite layer CALLED BY THE GUN (beam.zs header): the renderer puts it on that
gun's controller every frame, from that gun's measured muzzle, along its measured barrel, a voxel
rod per (pose, length), fading by a runtime translation over `rsqls_beam_len`. The spot is the
trace from the same muzzle along the same barrel. Melee hands draw nothing.

### Fixed this round (all measured by tools/gen_beams.py + tools/readyprobe)
- **The SSG had no beam**: the probe took Ready's first non-TNT1 frame (SHTN A, a 0-tic jump
  state); the gun sits in SHT2 A. The probe now walks the whole Ready sequence as the engine sets
  a layer (sprite "----"/"####", bSameFrame) and prints every frame on it (BD's Pistol: PIST E
  loaded, PIST I empty). A class may now rest in several poses: a row each, `PoseRow` picks.
- **Barrels of turned md3 frames**: BD's SSG.md3 frame 0 is the gun pitched 5 deg from frame 1
  (Kabsch fit, 0.03 cm). The barrel is the model's x axis only in the frame the mesh was BUILT in
  (`xlines`: most vertex runs along x); every other frame's barrel and muzzle come from the rigid
  motion carrying that frame onto it.
- **Recoil frames**: a frame the gun moved rigidly into (robust Kabsch, 95% of vertices, the
  muzzle end within 0.3 cm) is either still on the rest bore (joins it) or a kick of at most
  6 cm / 6 deg (a beam placement of its own). 106 placements, 7632 rods, pk3 7.4 MB; startup in
  the rig is within run-to-run noise of no laser (4.7-7.2 s either way).
- **Pump shotgun muzzle on the magazine tube**: the tip slab's middle sat between barrel and
  tube; a connected piece holding over half the slab's vertices is now the barrel (Shot_Gun muzzle
  U -0.98 -> +0.21 cm).

### Independent review, fixed
- Brutal Doom's brass and magazines are gravity projectiles owned by the player, so
  every BD gun turned into an arc after one shot. Now only a missile that leaves
  within 30 deg of the gun, from near the hand, faster than 6 u/tic counts. Rig,
  BD rifle on 17.3: ring, no arc.
- Learned per weapon AND fire mode (`#alt`): an alt-fire grenade never turns a
  primary into an arc. What is drawn is the primary.
- The spread is the 95th percentile of a histogram, not the widest ever (one melee
  kick or self-aimed shot no longer inflates it for good).
- The learner is a static handler: it keeps what it learned across map changes
  (a level handler is shut down on every unload). Not saved; re-learned after a load.

### Bugs found and fixed on the way
- **The muzzle sat on the grip on every gun since v1.** `GunOffset`'s parameter `upM`
  is the same name as the constant `UPM` to case-blind ZScript, so every offset was
  scaled by 0.03 instead of 34. Rig: default muzzle now 6 units ahead.
- **Pitch upside down** (shared with the grab): `Pitch()` negated AttackPitch, which is
  already positive-up; the muzzle offset and the no-hit end went the wrong way
  vertically. The hit point itself was right (the trace takes Doom's sense).
