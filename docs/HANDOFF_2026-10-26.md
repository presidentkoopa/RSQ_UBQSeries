regions + radial; six regions (2 hips slot 2, chest slot 3, chest/low back slot 4, two

## Current state (2026-10-27, read this first)

The sections below are the history of 10-26 and 10-27; much of what they describe is gone.
What the code is now:
- **Four gun holsters** (both hips, both shoulders: indices 0 1 4 5) and **two saber spots**
  (9 10). A holster holds exactly the gun put there; grip with a gun in hand puts it in (a full
  one swaps), grip with the hand empty draws. Each hand holsters and draws its own gun
  (`rsqtr_holster_off` / `rsqtr_draw_off` for the off hand). Dual is left to its own key
  (`rsqtr_pairs` 0).
- **Retired:** the chest, stomach, pectoral and lower-back places (indices 2 3 6 7 8 stay
  reserved, `RSQTR_Region.Retired`; their cvars are gone), the per-place slot lists
  (`rsqtr_rN_slots`), the radial, and doors (moved to RSQ_DistanceGrab). Holster profiles
  keep position and size per index; an old profile's fifth field (slots) is ignored.
- Torso (marine or Grunt), helmet (mirror-only), mirror, cigarette and profiles as in the
  README. Menu in BD22 VR Weapons' style.

## Review fixes (independent cold review, 2026-10-26)

- Synced fields are no longer wiped by OnRegister on load; loads are noticed on the first
  tic (doors released, orphan rings and icons from the save cleared).
- The cigarette count is carried on the player (`RSQTR_CigPack`), so "refill on a new
  level: off" works (an EventHandler is rebuilt on every map).
- The flicked butt starts where the hand was as sent in the event (it is a real,
  monster-drawing thing; the hand prop's position is only true on the holder's machine);
  a burnt-down butt drops in front of the player.
- Doors: one holder per door sector; the key is checked before taking over a moving
  door; the taken-over door's sound sequence is stopped; a holder who dies or leaves
  drops the door shut; a line whose special was cleared mid-hold drops rather than
  "commits"; drifting into a rig region no longer lets go of a held door.

## Open

- The userinfo has a size limit and every `user` cvar of every loaded mod counts; the rig has
  ~60. Region positions could move to `nosave` (only the local machine reads them) but that would
  drop everyone's saved places, so it waits for the owner's word.
- The second (off-hand) saber cannot be holstered: the rig acts on the main hand.

- Muzzle direction not readable by eye for: BrutalSMG, Rocket_Launcher, Plasma_Gun, Unmaker,
  GrenadeLauncher, BFG10k, Railgun, NukeLauncher, FlameCannon, HellishMissileLauncher, DSweap.
- Saved region positions are kept over new defaults (the pectorals' move shows after the
  menu's reset row, or moving them by hand).

- Not played on a headset. Feel numbers are first guesses: region places and sizes,
  door gain 2.5 (a 0.6 m lift opens ~50 units), the 7-unit touch distance, the pocket
  (0.12 forward, 0.16 left, 0.25 down, 0.09 radius), mouth (0.07 forward, 0.08 down,
  0.12 radius), Zippo flame distance 0.07 m, flick speed 1.5 m/s.
- A ceiling moved from script has no render interpolation (ZScript cannot set one), so a
  lifted door may step at 35 Hz in the headset.
- The rig's grip keys depend on `vr_secondary_button_mappings` (446 is the main grip
  only when it is 0); Use always works.
- Netplay: actions go through network events; the hand-held props follow the hand only
  on the owner's machine (other players see them at the pawn). Local-only cosmetic
  actors (rings, rumble emitters) follow the series' existing pattern.
