## F1: In the default Lash style a snap turn, or a quick turn of the head, can crack the whip
- Kind: possible bug
- Where: lash.zs:68-71 (the hand is measured against the eyes and turned by the pawn's angle), lash.zs:75 (the only jump guard: 24 units), lash.zs:87 (the flick test); whip.zs:869-873 (the code's own note: on QuestZDoom the pawn's angle follows the headset, and on the rig it took a 45 degree snap turn one tic before the controllers did); whip.zs:2793 (the Classic crack's guard for a turn tic, which the Lash style does not have)
- What the code does: every tic LashTick takes (hand - eye), turns it by minus the pawn's angle, and calls the change since last tic the hand's speed. A flick is a peak of 1.6 m/s in the last 4 tics followed by 40% of it or less. If the pawn's angle changes and the hand does not (a head turn, or the one tic a snap turn's angle leads the controllers), a still hand reads as moving: a hand 0.4 m from the eyes reads about 10 m/s for a tic on a 45 degree snap, then stops. That is a flick. The sweep's bearing (lash.zs:80, :100) is read the same way.
- Why it looks wrong: the whip would crack, and hit whatever the hand points at within 4.5 m, when the player only turned. The Classic whip was fixed for exactly this on 11-02 (whip.zs:153, "whip fires on snap turn") by carrying the thong with the body and skipping turn tics; the Lash style's reader was written later and has neither.
- Sure?: likely. A rig test settles it: whip in hand, hand still, snap turn; then turn the head fast.
- Fix A: in LashTick, drop the tic (and the last few samples) when the tracking space turned this tic (turnTic) or the pawn's angle changed by more than a few degrees (about 4 lines, 1 file).
- Fix B: measure the hand against the pawn in world axes and take the tracking space's turn out, as Carry does for the thong, so a head turn puts no speed into the hand (about 10 lines, 1 file).
- Or intended: the README says any fast change of the hand against your view is a flick, turns included.
