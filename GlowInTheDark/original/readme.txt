GlowInTheDark 1.1
Meant to compliment DarkDoom's Classic or Black settings.

Assembled and mostly written by PresidentKoopa.

October 2021.

Floor and Ceiling Glow should work Universally.
Liquid Glows Currently Supports 
Vanilla Doom and Brutal Doom 21 Only


MoreLights 2.3 shares a lot of commonalities with this, 
however importantly this will support BrutalDoom and, 
in theory, Oblige generated maps.
MoreLights supports Oblige, Vanilla (fewer liquids tho), 
Ancient Aliens, BTSX, OTEX, JazzJackrabbitDoom, Beautiful Doom,
and others.
