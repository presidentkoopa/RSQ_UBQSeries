RSQ Glow In The Dark -- credits
================================

The owner's Glow In The Dark 1.1 (October 2021), rewritten in ZScript for stock QuestZDoom 17.3 (ZScript and lumps
only, no engine changes). 1.1's own files are kept unmodified in the repo's original/ folder (not shipped).

GLOW IN THE DARK 1.1 -- presidentkoopa (the project owner)
  "Assembled and mostly written by PresidentKoopa." The idea, the menu, the floor/ceiling glow script as he modified
  it, and the Glow list: every liquid in Doom, the HD liquids and Brutal Doom's splashes ("Which involved a lot of
  typing. Ugh."). That list ships unchanged here as GLDEFS.txt.

WALL GLOW -- after ColorfulMaps
  1.1's floor/ceiling glow script was "extensively modified from a modified offshoot of ColorfulMaps (initially by
  Solid_Head)", specifically Colorfulmaps_v3.pke aka "Lite" by NightFright (page 3 of the ZDoom forum thread,
  https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?t=66484&f=43). This pk3 carries no ACS; the ZScript is new, the behaviour is
  theirs and the owner's.

GLOWING LIQUIDS -- after GlowingLiquids
  1.1's liquid glow was "built off of GlowingLiquids by Finder153"
  (https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?f=46&t=69911); its GLDEFS list was extensively added to by the owner.

MORE LIGHTS -- Jakie (GitHub: SnakieJakie)
  https://github.com/SnakieJakie/More-Lights (ZDoom forum: https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?f=46&t=72206).
  Glowing flats and dynamic lights for GZDoom/Zandronum; discontinued by its author on 18 August 2023 (abandoned).
  The owner: Glow In The Dark changed because he added More Lights to it. 1.1's own readme says More Lights 2.3
  "shares a lot of commonalities with this" and recommends playing them together.
  Licence (the repo's README, as of 2026-11-06): "I (Jakie) is giving you full permission to use More Lights for any
  projects. No need to ask me. Have fun!" and "Anyone is free to download the last version and build off of my
  work, even without credit." (Its earlier "Jakie License" (c) 2022 Jakie -- use, copy, modify, merge, publish,
  distribute and sublicense, but not sell -- was deleted by the author on 2023-08-19.) Credited here all the same.
  Checked 2026-11-06 against the repo (Src/filter/doom.id, doom.freedoom): this pk3 ships no More Lights file, no
  dynamic lights and none of its own glowing-flat entries (light panels, computer flats, teleporters); the 54 flat
  names both lists share are Doom's stock liquids.

RS RADIANCE -- presidentkoopa
  The owner's later direction for this concept (UZDXREMA): colour policies in a seeded window, seamless rooms,
  waves, looks, reactive lighting. Ideas carried over where QuestZDoom 17.3 allows; no code copied.

WRITTEN FOR THIS PACK
  All ZScript (zscript/rsqgd): the map-start pass, profiles, the writer, living liquids, the things that matter,
  the reactions, the service, the menu rows. tools/gen_liquids.py.
  "Subdued" light style: the idea of the Subdued Lights mod (double radius, half brightness), applied to this
  mod's own few lights only; nothing of that mod is used.

PLAYS WELL WITH (not bundled, not changed)
  DarkDoomZ by Caligari87 (Dark Doom concept by Josh771). This mod writes glow colours, not what DarkDoomZ owns
  (light levels, fog, its flashlight); during a miniboss it lowers DarkDoomZ's own Post-Gain setting and puts it
  back, and a menu row can apply a profile's DarkDoomZ Mode/Preset.
  1.1's readme also recommended More Lights (above), Lights2.wad, Subtle Lights and SynthDoom Vol4.
