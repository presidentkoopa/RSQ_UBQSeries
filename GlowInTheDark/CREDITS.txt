RSQ Glow In The Dark -- credits
================================

The owner's Glow In The Dark 1.1 (October 2021), rewritten in ZScript for stock QuestZDoom 17.3 (ZScript and lumps
only, no engine changes). 1.1's own files are kept unmodified in the repo's original/ folder (not shipped).

GLOW IN THE DARK 1.1 -- presidentkoopa (the project owner)
  "Assembled and mostly written by PresidentKoopa." The idea, the menu, the floor/ceiling glow script as he modified
  it, and the Glow list: every liquid in Doom, the HD liquids and Brutal Doom's splashes ("Which involved a lot of
  typing. Ugh."). That list ships unchanged here as GLDEFS.txt.

WALL GLOW -- after ColorfulMaps
  1.1's floor/ceiling glow script was "extensively modified from a modified offshoot of ColorfulMaps (initially by
  Solid_Head)", specifically Colorfulmaps_v3.pke aka "Lite" by NightFright (page 3 of the ZDoom forum thread,
  https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?t=66484&f=43). This pk3 carries no ACS; the ZScript is new, the behaviour is
  theirs and the owner's.

GLOWING LIQUIDS -- after GlowingLiquids
  1.1's liquid glow was "built off of GlowingLiquids by Finder153"
  (https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?f=46&t=69911); its GLDEFS list was extensively added to by the owner.

WRITTEN FOR THIS PACK
  The ZScript (zscript/rsqgd): the map-start pass, the switches, the service. tools/gen_liquids.py.

PLAYS WELL WITH (not bundled, not changed)
  DarkDoomZ by Caligari87 (Dark Doom concept by Josh771). This mod never touches what DarkDoomZ changes (sector light
  levels, fog density, its flashlight): it only writes the floor/ceiling glow of each sector.
  1.1's readme also recommended MoreLights, Lights2.wad, Subtle Lights and SynthDoom Vol4.
