// THE OWNER'S PUNCH SOUNDS (2026-11-16, design/MELEE_SOUNDS.md; his uploads punch1-punch11, from Streets of Rage 2 (Sega, 1992)): one baked
// file per hit (trim, pitch, layers, gain and fades all in it; Melee/tools/meleesounds.py is the recipe). gloves.zs picks
// by the punch: the jab rotates jab1 > jab2 > jab3 at +/-5% pitch drawn as it plays, a hook is hookr (right hand) or
// hookl (left), the uppercut is the kick's sound. club.zs / shove.zs: the pistol whip and the gun-butt shove. swing is
// the whoosh as a fist swing arms. The SuperPunchOut sounds above stay for the Hadouken, the swat-back, the guard.

rsqml/jab2       "sounds/rsqml/hit/jab2.ogg"        // punch6
rsqml/jab3       "sounds/rsqml/hit/jab3.ogg"        // punch1
rsqml/hookr      "sounds/rsqml/hit/hookr.ogg"       // punch11
rsqml/hookl      "sounds/rsqml/hit/hookl.ogg"       // punch5
