RSQ_Melee -- credits
====================

Code: ZScript for the Radiant Silvergun Quest series (presidentkoopa), for stock QuestZDoom 17.3 by
Emawind84 and Team Beef. No engine change. Moved together on 2026-11-01 from three series mods; every line
below comes with the code it credits.

THE BOXING GLOVES AND THE PUNCH (from RSQ Gun Bonsai VR)
  Gloves, punch reader and sounds from SuperPunchOut (presidentkoopa): the glove weapons, the Arcade punch
  reader and ClassifyPunch, Star Punch's banking rule. The glove mesh and its fit are Ermac's (iAmErmac,
  PunchOutVR / Virtual Punch Out); punch and star sounds from Punch Out Doom (DerrickDavison) through
  Virtual Punch Out, as SuperPunchOut ships them. Punch-Out!! is Nintendo's.
  A glove's punch is Brutal Doom's own fist numbers (MeleeStrikeNormal 10, MeleeStrike2 20; brutal22test6
  Melee.dec), parsed, not shipped.
  Guard, Hadouken (Return Fire), Wall Splat and Heavy Hands: the owner's picks 52-55; what each became is from
  the Quest teardown (presidentkoopa's RSQ_QuestEngineSurvey: surveys/01_MELEE.md 3.6, 4.4, 4.5, 4.7, 4.11;
  apply/02_MELEE_BUILD.md). Their upgrades and numbers stay in RSQ Gun Bonsai VR, an addon for Rebecca
  "ToxicFrog" Kelly's Gun Bonsai (MIT).

THE GUN-BUTT SHOVE AND ITS SLOW-MO (from RSQ Gestures)
  The gun butt shove is BD22 VR Reloaded's butt shove (the owner's own mod; zscript/rsvr/melee.zs: the swing
  speed, reach, wait and the blow it lands), ported. Numbers taken from Brutal Doom (Sergeant Mark IV;
  brutal22test6.pk3): KickAttack's damage and alert range (Melee.dec) and the monsters' Pain.Kick thrusts
  (actors/Enemies). Sounds played by name, NOT shipped: Brutal Doom's Melee/SwingStrong and Kick/flesh when it
  is loaded, otherwise stock Doom's skeleton/swing and punch (id Software). The shotgun window's blood is
  each monster's own blood type (Brutal Doom's when it is loaded). Slow motion is RSQ_SlowMo's (rsm_pulse);
  the award is RSQ_Score's (RS_ScoreService). The swing reader is RSQ Gestures' punch reader (a port of
  presidentkoopa's RS_Gestures), copied.

THE HEADLOCK (from RSQ Distance Grab)
  The Headlock, Human Shield and the headlock throw: RSQ Distance Grab's (presidentkoopa; its feel ported from
  his desktop RS_WorldHands). Without Distance Grab, Melee's own toss uses Distance Grab's default throw
  numbers and impact rule.

MELEE'S HANDS (2026-11-02)
  Hand model by Ermac (iAmErmac), Rusted Legacy / Doom Force Unleashed, MIT licence: models/hand/hand.md3 (its
  frames 0 and 1, cut by Melee/tools/md3_frames.py) and hand.png, at Ermac's own fit (modeldefs/weapons/fist.txt).
  The licence text: credits/Ermac_RustedLegacy_LICENSE.txt.
  The FBX hand: BD22 VR's free hand (RS_Brutal22WeaponsQ, presidentkoopa: src/models/rsq/fbx/fbx_oh_free_R.md3,
  frames 0 and 1, and fbx_hand.png), baked there from the owner's own hand model, at BD22 VR's fit. Its skin tones
  are BD22 VR's own textures, read from BD22 VR's pk3 when it is loaded (none shipped here).

HIT SPARKS (2026-11-02)
  Capcom vs. SNK 2 hit sparks, ripped and converted by Phantom.of.the.Server; property of Capcom, non-commercial.
  (Normal, Special and Super Spark, all four angles of each, and the Guard sparks; credits/CvS2hitsparks_Readme.txt.)
  Random Sprite Rips, ripped by Phantom.of.the.Server: "This is just a compilation of some stuff I ripped for my
  chars along the time. Use it freely. The sprites are property of their respective game companies, so they cannot
  be used for any commercial purposes." (the Hadouken hit burst; credits/RandomSpriteRips_Readme.txt).
  Converted by Melee/tools/sparks.py (keyed, cropped, shrunk; nothing repainted).

FISTS TREE SOUNDS (2026-11-02)
  From the owner's two sound wells, RS Combat FX and RS Ballistics (presidentkoopa's libraries), one file at a time;
  Melee/tools/fxsounds.py is the record of each pull (the well and the path) and converts each to mono 44.1 kHz.

IMPACT BURSTS (2026-11-02)
  "VFX Hit Impact" by kiddolink, a paid asset pack bought by presidentkoopa for this series (the five 96x96 impacts,
  vfx_hit_effects-Sheet.png). Cropped and, for the elements, recoloured (mid tones to the element's colour, highlights
  and alpha kept) by Melee/tools/bursts.py; nothing repainted. Used under the purchase's licence; not for re-sale or
  redistribution as an asset pack.
  Sounds played by name, not shipped: stock Doom 2's DSTINK (the guard) and DSSKEPCH (the swat).

The shared Radiant Silvergun Options menu is the series' tools/rsmenu; RSQ_Accessibility's settings are read
through Accessibility/tools/a11y_template.zs.
