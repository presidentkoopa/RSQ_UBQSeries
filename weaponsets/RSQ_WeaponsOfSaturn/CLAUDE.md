# RSQ_WeaponsOfSaturn — rules for every session

Ground-up VR rebuild of Weapons of Saturn in ZScript for QuestZDoom 17.3. The agreed spec is SPEC.md; it is the
owner's, change it only on his word.

1. Questions go to the owner. Never decide a design, mesh, sound or behaviour he has not decided; ask.
2. A mesh, hand fit or measurement below the BD22 VR standard is reported to the owner, not worked around.
3. No ACS. No engine changes. QZD 17.3 / ZScript 4.14 APIs only (RSQ_QuestEngineSurvey for what exists).
4. Nothing is "working" until the rig suite shows it. No pk3 leaves without a passing suite; the build enforces it.
5. One rig suite at the end, unattended; one report with the logs. No piecemeal rig chatter.
6. Generic Weapons and BD22 VR are out of scope until the owner says.
